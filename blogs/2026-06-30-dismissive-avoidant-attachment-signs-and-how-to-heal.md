Written by: Jessica Migala Medically Reviewed by: Chaitanya Pabbati, MD Have you ever reacted to a partner, family member, or friend expressing their needs by telling them they’re being too sensitive or dramatic? You may be someone with a dismissive avoidant attachment style. This style is often hallmarked by avoiding closeness and vulnerability.
