Written by Sarah Bence, OTR/L Medically reviewed by Chaitanya Pabbati, MD Body dysmorphic disorder (BDD) isn’t just a beauty obsession or self-esteem problem. It’s a diagnosable mental health condition that, when left untreated, can have devastating impacts on a person’s life. Body dysmorphia treatment often involves a combination of therapy and medication.
